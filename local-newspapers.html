The news that never reaches Dhaka

A union parishad dispute, a bridge left unfinished for three years, a local college's exam results, a river eroding a particular village, the price of produce at a specific market — these shape daily life for the people involved and will almost never earn space in a national daily.

Regional newspapers exist for exactly this. Chattogram's Dainik Azadi, published since 1960, is among the oldest and best known, and most divisions sustain their own established titles alongside a larger number of district weeklies and online outlets.

They also function as a feeder system. A story that starts in a district paper is sometimes picked up by a national desk days later, by which point it has already been documented locally by a reporter who knows the place.

Working under pressure

Local journalism in Bangladesh is demanding work. Revenue is thin, newsroom staff are few, and reporters often live in the same small community as the people their reporting affects, which makes covering local power considerably harder than it is from a capital-city desk.

That proximity cuts both ways. It produces reporting with genuine knowledge of local history and personalities that no parachuting correspondent can match — and it makes independence harder to maintain. Readers of the local press learn quickly which outlets have earned trust in their own area.

Many regional papers now publish online as well as in print, which has extended their reach to district residents working in Dhaka or abroad who want to follow news from home.

বাংলাদেশের প্রতিটি বিভাগেই নিজস্ব দৈনিক ও সাপ্তাহিক পত্রিকা আছে। চট্টগ্রাম, সিলেট, রাজশাহী, খুলনা, রংপুর, বরিশাল, ময়মনসিংহ ও ঢাকার আঞ্চলিক পত্রিকাগুলো স্থানীয় প্রশাসন, শিক্ষা, কৃষি, নদীভাঙন ও স্থানীয় রাজনীতির খবর নিয়মিত প্রকাশ করে।

প্রবাসে বা ঢাকায় থাকা মানুষের কাছে এই পত্রিকাগুলোর আলাদা গুরুত্ব আছে — নিজের জেলার খবর জাতীয় দৈনিকে সাধারণত আসে না, কিন্তু আঞ্চলিক পত্রিকায় প্রতিদিনই থাকে।

এই তালিকায় যে বিভাগের পত্রিকা যুক্ত আছে, সেগুলো বিভাগ অনুযায়ী চিহ্নিত করা হয়েছে। আপনার জেলার কোনো পত্রিকা এখানে না থাকলে নাম ও ওয়েবসাইটসহ আমাদের জানাতে পারেন।

FAQ

Why read a local newspaper when national dailies cover the country?

National papers cover districts only when something large happens. Routine local administration, education, agriculture and infrastructure news — the things that affect daily life in a specific area — appear almost exclusively in the regional press.

Which is the oldest regional newspaper in Bangladesh?

Dainik Azadi of Chattogram, published since 1960, is among the oldest continuously running regional dailies and remains one of the most prominent outside Dhaka.
