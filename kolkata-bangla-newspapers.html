Kolkata & West Bengal Bangla Newspapers

Bangla is read on both sides of the border, and the Kolkata press has its own long tradition, its own style and its own concerns. For many readers in Bangladesh it is a familiar second window on the language.

A parallel tradition

Calcutta was the birthplace of Bangla journalism, and several Kolkata titles trace their history back a century or more — Anandabazar Patrika, founded in 1922, being the best known. Others such as Bartaman, Sangbad Pratidin, Aajkaal and Ei Samay serve a large daily readership across West Bengal.

Their news agenda is naturally Indian: state politics in West Bengal, national politics in Delhi, the Indian economy, Bollywood and Tollywood, and Indian cricket. Bangladesh appears mainly when the two countries intersect — trade, rivers, borders, cricket.

For Bangladeshi readers the attraction is usually cultural rather than political: literature, film, music, the Durga Puja magazine editions, and sport.

ভাষার পার্থক্য

একই ভাষা হলেও দুই পাড়ের সংবাদপত্রের বাংলা হুবহু এক নয়। শব্দচয়ন, বানানরীতি ও বাক্যগঠনে পার্থক্য আছে — কলকাতার পত্রিকায় কিছু শব্দ ব্যবহৃত হয় যা বাংলাদেশে কম প্রচলিত, আবার উল্টোটাও সত্য।

সংখ্যা, তারিখ ও পরিমাপের প্রকাশভঙ্গিতেও পার্থক্য চোখে পড়ে। ভাষা নিয়ে আগ্রহ থাকলে একই ঘটনার খবর দুই দেশের পত্রিকায় পড়ে তুলনা করা বেশ উপভোগ্য একটি অভ্যাস।

সাহিত্য, চলচ্চিত্র ও পূজা সংখ্যার জন্য কলকাতার পত্রিকাগুলোর আলাদা ঐতিহ্য রয়েছে, যা বাংলাদেশের পাঠকদের কাছেও বহু বছর ধরে জনপ্রিয়।

Reading across the border

When a story involves both countries — a river-water agreement, a border incident, a trade decision, a cricket series — reading the Dhaka and Kolkata coverage side by side is instructive. The facts usually match; the framing, the choice of quotes and the sense of what matters often do not.

That contrast is one of the more useful habits a reader can develop, and it applies well beyond these two presses.

FAQ

Which is the largest Bangla newspaper in Kolkata?

Anandabazar Patrika, published since 1922, is generally regarded as the largest-circulation Bangla daily in West Bengal, with Bartaman, Sangbad Pratidin and Ei Samay among the other major titles.

Is the Bangla used in Kolkata papers different?

The language is the same, but vocabulary, spelling conventions and sentence style differ in noticeable ways between the Kolkata and Dhaka press. Most readers adjust within a few articles.

Can I read these papers from Bangladesh?

Yes, the websites are open to read from anywhere. A few carry subscription prompts for magazine or archive sections.
