e-Papers · Digital Print Editions

An e-paper is not a news website. It is the printed newspaper itself, reproduced page by page, with the same layout, headlines, photographs and advertisements — which makes it useful for things a news website handles poorly.

What an e-paper actually is

When a newspaper is laid out for print, every item has a fixed position on a numbered page. The e-paper preserves exactly that: you turn pages rather than scroll a feed, and a story appears at the size and position the editors gave it.

That page position carries information a website strips away. A report at the top of page one was judged more important than one at the bottom of page five, and on an e-paper you can see that judgement at a glance. The web edition of the same paper flattens everything into a list.

Most Bangladeshi dailies publish the day's e-paper free each morning, with a window of recent back issues. Some keep longer archives behind free registration.

When the e-paper is the right tool

Job circulars and tender notices. These are published as formal printed notices with exact wording, deadlines and reference numbers, and they frequently never appear on the news website at all. For government recruitment and procurement, the e-paper is often the only full source.

Proof of publication. If you need to show that a notice, a legal announcement or a result was published on a particular date, the dated page of an e-paper is the record.

Readers abroad. For Bangladeshis living overseas, the e-paper is the closest thing to the morning paper they grew up with, available at the same time as in Dhaka.

Archive work. Students, researchers and journalists checking how an event was reported on the day it happened need the page as printed, not a later web rewrite.

চাকরির বিজ্ঞপ্তি বা টেন্ডার নোটিশ পেলে পৃষ্ঠার স্ক্রিনশট বা পিডিএফ সংরক্ষণ করে রাখুন — পরদিন নতুন সংস্করণ এলে আগেরটি খুঁজে পাওয়া কঠিন হতে পারে।

FAQ

Is the e-paper the same as the newspaper's website?

No. The website is written for the screen and updated all day; the e-paper is the printed edition reproduced page by page, fixed as it was printed that morning. Many stories appear in only one of the two.

Can I read yesterday's or last week's e-paper?

Usually yes. Most papers keep a short archive of recent editions, though how far back it goes differs by publisher and some require free registration for older issues.

Why do people use e-papers for job circulars?

Government and corporate recruitment notices are published as formal printed advertisements with exact deadlines and reference numbers, and they often never appear on the news website. The e-paper page is the complete, citable version.
