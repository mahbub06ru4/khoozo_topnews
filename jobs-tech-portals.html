Jobs, Tech & Government Portals

Job circulars in Bangladesh are scattered across newspaper advertisements, government portals and private job boards. This page explains where each kind is published and links to the official sources.

Government recruitment

Public sector recruitment follows a formal process. A circular is published as a printed advertisement and on the recruiting authority's own website, applications are usually submitted through an official online portal, and admit cards and results are released the same way.

The Teletalk-operated jobs portal handles applications for a large share of government positions, and the Bangladesh Public Service Commission runs the BCS examination and other cadre recruitment on its own system. The national information portal links out to individual ministry and department sites.

Because the newspaper advertisement is the formal notice, the e-paper edition of the dailies is often where a circular appears in full, with the exact deadline, reference number and conditions. That is worth checking against any summary you see elsewhere.

Private sector hiring

Private recruitment runs mostly through job boards, with Bdjobs the largest and longest-established. Employers post there directly, applications are made through the site, and most listings carry a visible closing date.

Newspaper classified pages still matter for certain sectors, and many national dailies maintain a dedicated jobs section on their website that reproduces those listings.

Technology portals such as TechShohor and Techtunes cover the local technology industry, which is useful for anyone tracking where hiring in software and IT services is actually happening.

চাকরির বিজ্ঞপ্তি নিয়ে সতর্কতা

চাকরির নামে প্রতারণা বাংলাদেশে একটি বাস্তব সমস্যা। মনে রাখবেন, সরকারি চাকরির আবেদন ফি নির্দিষ্ট অফিশিয়াল পদ্ধতিতেই জমা দিতে হয় — ব্যক্তিগত বিকাশ বা নগদ নম্বরে টাকা চাইলে সেটি প্রতারণা।

নিয়োগের নিশ্চয়তা দিয়ে কেউ টাকা চাইলে তা বিশ্বাস করবেন না। বৈধ কোনো প্রতিষ্ঠান নিয়োগের বিনিময়ে অর্থ দাবি করে না।

বিজ্ঞপ্তিটি সবসময় সংশ্লিষ্ট দপ্তরের নিজস্ব ওয়েবসাইট বা পত্রিকার ই-পেপারে মিলিয়ে দেখুন। ফেসবুকে ছড়ানো স্ক্রিনশটে পুরোনো বা বানানো বিজ্ঞপ্তি থাকতে পারে, আর আবেদনের শেষ তারিখ প্রায়ই বদলে দেওয়া হয়।

FAQ

Where are government job circulars officially published?

On the recruiting authority's own website and as printed newspaper advertisements, with applications generally submitted through the official Teletalk jobs portal or the PSC's own system for cadre recruitment.

Is there a fee to apply for a government job?

Most government applications carry an official examination fee, but it is paid only through the prescribed official channel stated in the circular. Anyone asking you to send money to a personal mobile wallet number is attempting fraud.

Why check the e-paper for a job circular?

The newspaper advertisement is the formal notice and carries the complete wording, deadline and reference number. Summaries circulating on social media often omit conditions or show an outdated deadline.
