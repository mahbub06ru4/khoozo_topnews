About Top News by Khoozo

A fast, mobile-first gateway to every major Bangladeshi newspaper — built by Khoozo.

What we do

Top News is a curated directory of Bangladeshi and international news websites. National Bangla dailies, online news portals, TV news channels, e-papers, English dailies, divisional newspapers and job & tech portals are organised into clear categories so readers can reach the right publisher in a single tap.

A small part of the homepage shows live headlines collected automatically from Google News Bangladesh. We display only the headline, the publisher name and the publication time, and every headline links to the original article on the publisher's own website.

আমাদের সম্পর্কে

Top News by Khoozo বাংলাদেশের পাঠকদের জন্য তৈরি একটি দ্রুতগতির সংবাদপত্র ডিরেক্টরি। জাতীয় দৈনিক, অনলাইন পোর্টাল, টিভি নিউজ, ই-পেপার, ইংরেজি দৈনিক ও বিভাগীয় পত্রিকা এখানে বিষয়ভিত্তিকভাবে সাজানো। কম ইন্টারনেট ডেটাতেও যেন দ্রুত খোলে, সেভাবেই সাইটটি তৈরি করা হয়েছে।

Our principles

  • Original sources first. We never copy or rewrite articles — we send traffic to the publishers who do the reporting.
  • Speed and accessibility. The site is a lightweight static website designed for low-cost mobile data and older phones.
  • Neutrality. Listing a publisher is not an endorsement of its editorial positions. We aim to cover the full range of established Bangladeshi media.
  • Accuracy. Links are reviewed periodically. If you find a broken or incorrect link, please tell us.

Who is behind Top News?

Top News is a product of Khoozo, a Bangladeshi digital products company that also builds the Khoozo App and Khoozo Shop. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps it free for every reader.
