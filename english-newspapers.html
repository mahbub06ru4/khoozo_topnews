English Newspapers of Bangladesh

Bangladesh's English-language press is small in circulation but carries weight well beyond its numbers. It is how much of the outside world reads the country, and how a good deal of policy and business argument gets conducted.

A press with a particular audience

English dailies in Bangladesh were never aimed at the mass market. Their readership concentrates among civil servants, diplomats, development and NGO staff, corporate management, university students and Bangladeshis living abroad — a small share of the population that holds a disproportionate amount of decision-making.

That shapes the journalism. English papers typically devote more space to economic policy, trade, foreign affairs, governance and long-form investigation, and less to the local-court and district reporting that fills the Bangla press. The Daily Star, established in the early 1990s, is the most prominent, with Dhaka Tribune, New Age, The Financial Express, The Business Standard and others serving overlapping audiences.

Because international wire services, embassies and foreign correspondents follow these papers closely, a story that breaks in the English press often travels abroad faster than a larger story in a Bangla daily.

Business and economic reporting

Some of the most specialised journalism in the country sits in the English-language business press: ready-made garment exports and buyer behaviour, banking and non-performing loans, energy pricing, capital markets, remittance flows and the annual budget.

For anyone studying or working in these areas, these papers are a primary source rather than a convenience. They publish figures, quote officials on the record and track policy changes in detail that general-interest coverage compresses into a sentence.

FAQ

Which is the leading English newspaper in Bangladesh?

The Daily Star, founded in 1991, is generally considered the most widely read English daily, with Dhaka Tribune, New Age, The Financial Express and The Business Standard also established national titles.

Are English papers just translations of the Bangla ones?

No. They are independent newsrooms with their own reporters and editorial priorities. Coverage of the same event frequently differs in emphasis, detail and framing between the two language presses.

Which paper is best for business news?

The Financial Express and The Business Standard specialise in economic and financial coverage, while the general English dailies maintain substantial business desks. For a specific sector it is worth comparing two of them.
