Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Top News by Khoozo ("Top News", "we", "us"), available at topnews.khoozo.com and operated by Khoozo, handles information when you visit the website.

1. Information we collect

Top News does not require registration and we do not ask for your name, phone number or other personal details. The website itself stores only two small preferences in your browser:

  • Theme preference (light or dark mode) in local storage.
  • Mobile banner closed state in session storage, so a closed banner stays closed during your visit.

These values never leave your device and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

2. Log files and hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request for security and operational purposes. Please see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use (or may use) Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and other regions where consent is required will be shown a consent message before personalised ads are served.

4. Third-party content

Newspaper logos are loaded from Google's public favicon service, fonts from Google Fonts, and live headlines link to Google News and the original publishers. When you click an external link, you leave Top News and the privacy policy of that website applies.

5. Children's privacy

Top News is a general-audience news directory. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data directly, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us and we will respond as soon as possible.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.

8. Contact

Email: support@khoozo.com · Website: khoozo.com
